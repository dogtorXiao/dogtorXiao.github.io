我是多伦多大学物理系的博士研究生，师从 Dylan Jones 教授，预计 2026 年毕业。研究聚焦温室气体与大气污染的排放量化：用 WRF-XSTILT 追踪 CO₂ 的传输来源，用高分辨率 WRF-LES 模拟烟羽并结合观测反演电厂排放，也在尝试用机器学习模拟烟羽。

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Physics, University of Toronto, advised by Prof. Dylan Jones and expecting to graduate in 2026. My research quantifies greenhouse gas and air-pollution emissions: I use WRF-XSTILT to trace where CO₂ comes from, high-resolution WRF-LES to simulate plumes and infer power-plant emissions from observations, and I am also exploring the use of Machine Learning for emulating point source plumes.

研究方向Research 大气传输、温室气体排放反演、数据同化与机器学习应用。 Atmospheric transport, greenhouse gas emission inversion, data assimilation, and machine learning.
